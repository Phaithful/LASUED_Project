DIRECTORATE OF ENTREPRENEURSHIP AND SKILL DEVELOPMENT

Lagos State University of Education's Directorate of Entrepreneurship and Skills Development, in partnership with the Ministry of Education, is transforming education in Lagos. We're building a database, certifying professionals, and empowering students with entrepreneurship. Join us in shaping the future of education, one step at a time.

Our Main Aim

Our main aim is to revolutionize education in Lagos by integrating structured databases, certifying professionals, and empowering students with entrepreneurial skills.

What We Offer

We offer a comprehensive approach to education, including database management, professional certification, and student entrepreneurship programs, all aimed at enhancing the educational landscape in Lagos.

Who We Are

The Directorate of Entrepreneurship and Skills Development (DESD), approved by the Senate and Council of the University in May 2023, and designed to be a world-class, life-long entrepreneurship learning and development center.

Lagos State University of Education (LASUED), as a new University established only in 2022, have deliberately positioned itself to apply unconventional solutions to address the numerous problems of lack of creative, innovative and entrepreneurial skills among our teeming youth, which has led to the huge state of unemployment and joblessness among young Nigerians.

In addition, we are resolve to apply ourselves to proffering solutions to the business and economic challenges of our state and nation through entrepreneurship education development.

To achieve the above, we are resolved to partner with the corporate organizations around us, as the ultimate consumers of our products (the graduates) in producing the types of graduates that will add value to their organizations and change the dynamics of administration of the nation. This unique vision we strategically set out to achieve through the DESD.
